About

I am an Industrial Engineer with a Licentiate in Engineering Sciences from the Universidad de O’Higgins and a Minor in Data Analytics. I combine data analytics, artificial intelligence and project management to turn data into solutions that create value in industrial and strategic contexts.

I design end-to-end data solutions: data engineering (ETL/ELT processes, data modeling and quality), computer vision and machine learning models taken to production, applying MLOps, DataOps and DevOps so that solutions are reliable, measurable and maintainable.

With a research background presented at international conferences and experience leading teams, I am looking to collaborate in projects that require a systemic view, analytical skills and commitment to technological innovation applied to industry.

Areas of expertise

Data & analytics

Data engineering and modeling, ETL/ELT processes and analytics for data-driven decisions.

AI & computer vision

Machine learning and deep learning models, from research to deployment.

MLOps & DevOps

Automation, versioning and monitoring to take models to production reliably.

Project & product management

Roadmaps, backlog and coordination of teams and stakeholders with agile methodologies.
